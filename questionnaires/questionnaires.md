# Mystery shopping questionnaires

Scoring for every criterion: **2 = Yes / fully met · 1 = Partly met · 0 = No / not met**. Leave blank if not observed.

## Email

*Scenarios:* Filing procedure enquiry, Court fee enquiry, Request for case status guidance, Document requirements for a claim  
*Response time:* minutes to first reply (illustrative standard ≤ 1440 min)

| ID | Dimension | Question |
|---|---|---|
| RT1 | Response time | Was the first response received within the service standard for this channel? |
| RT2 | Response time | Was an expected resolution time communicated? |
| CQ1 | Communication quality | Was the response clear and free of unexplained jargon? |
| CQ2 | Communication quality | Was the response offered or provided in the customer's preferred language (Arabic / English)? |
| CQ3 | Communication quality | Was the written response well structured and free of errors? |
| PR1 | Professionalism | Was the customer greeted courteously and addressed respectfully? |
| PR2 | Professionalism | Did the staff member identify themselves (name or ID)? |
| PR3 | Professionalism | Did the staff member keep a neutral, impartial tone (no legal advice given)? |
| IA1 | Information accuracy | Was the information provided correct against the published procedure? |
| IA2 | Information accuracy | Was the information complete (documents, fees, timelines)? |
| IA3 | Information accuracy | Was the customer directed to the correct official page, form or e-service? |
| RE1 | Resolution effectiveness | Was the enquiry resolved at first contact? |
| RE2 | Resolution effectiveness | If not resolved, was a clear next step or referral given? |
| CX1 | Customer experience | Was the environment / channel easy to use (signage, menus, accessibility)? |
| CX2 | Customer experience | Did the interaction feel efficient overall? |

Also record: likelihood to recommend (0-10) and a factual overall observation.

## Registry and ancillary services: additional criteria

In-person channels also score **CQ4** (active listening), **PR2** (staff identification) and **RE3** (transaction completed correctly: documents accepted, receipt issued). Waiting time is measured from arrival or ticket issue to being served.
